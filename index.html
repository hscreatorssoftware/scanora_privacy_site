AT A GLANCE

Privacy built around local scanning

Scanora does not require you to create an account. QR and barcode scans, generated codes, OCR text, document scans, history and preferences are designed to stay on your device unless you choose to export or share them. To stay free, the app shows ads and uses Google services for ads, diagnostics, notifications and purchases, explained in plain language below.

01

Local-first workspace

Your saved history, generated codes, OCR results and Scanora-managed documents are stored locally in the app or device storage.

02

No account required

Scanora does not require registration and does not ask you to create a Scanora profile to use its core features.

03

You control sharing

Content leaves Scanora only when you intentionally use actions such as Share, Save to device, Open in another app, email or SMS.

04

Free with ads

Scanora shows Google AdMob ads. Where the law requires it, Google asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice later in Settings.

05

Scans stay out of analytics

Firebase receives technical diagnostics and basic usage events such as which screen opened or which type of code was scanned. It does not receive the scanned value, links, Wi-Fi details, OCR text or documents.

06

Optional Premium

You can remove ads with a monthly, yearly or lifetime purchase handled by Google Play. Scanora never sees your payment card details.

DATA & PERMISSIONS

What Scanora processes

Permissions and files are used only to provide the scanning, recognition, saving and sharing features you request.

Simple ruleScanora does not require an account and does not operate a developer cloud service for your scanned content. Ads, diagnostics, notifications and purchases use Google services, see Services and Ads & analytics.

Camera

Scanora may request camera access to scan QR codes and barcodes. Camera frames are analyzed for code detection while you use the scanner. Scanora does not operate a developer server that receives your live camera feed.

Images and gallery selection

When you choose an image for gallery scanning, OCR or document-related workflows, Android's system picker or applicable storage access is used to access the file you select. Scanora processes the selected content for the feature you requested.

OCR text

Text recognition uses Google ML Kit on the device. Recognized text may be saved to your Scanora Library when you choose to keep it, and may be copied, shared or exported only when you request those actions.

Document scans and PDFs

Document scanning uses the Google ML Kit document scanner. Scanora can keep generated PDF documents in its local Library and can export copies to a device location you select. Google Play services may download scanner components required for this feature.

QR codes and barcodes

Values you scan or generate can include text, URLs, phone numbers, email details, Wi-Fi credentials, contact information and other code payloads. These values are handled locally and may appear in your local history until you delete them or clear app data.

App preferences

Preferences such as theme, language, onboarding state, scan sound, haptic feedback and similar settings may be stored locally using Android app storage.

Network access

Scanora uses the internet connection and network state to load ads, send diagnostics, fetch app settings, receive notifications, check for updates, complete purchases and open links that you choose to open. Your scanned content is not uploaded to a Scanora server.

Advertising ID permission

On Android 13 and newer, Scanora declares the advertising ID permission so Google AdMob can serve and measure ads. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android and Google settings, and you can remove ads with Premium.

Notifications

On Android 13 and newer, Scanora asks once whether it may show notifications about updates, offers, new features and service status. You can say no or turn notifications off at any time in Android Settings. Scanora keeps working either way.

Legacy storage permission

On Android 9 and below, Scanora may request storage permission when required to save exported images. On newer Android versions, modern scoped-storage and system-picker flows are used where applicable.

SERVICE PROVIDERS

Google services used by Scanora

Some app features depend on Google-provided Android components and online services. Their processing is governed by Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

Google ML Kit

Used for barcode scanning, text recognition and document scanning. Depending on the feature and installed components, ML Kit or Google Play services may process limited device and technical information necessary to deliver the service.

Google Play in-app updates

Scanora may check Google Play for app updates and show an update flow. Google Play handles update availability, download and installation under Google's own policies.

Google AdMob & consent

Scanora shows banner, native, interstitial, rewarded and app-open ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Google's User Messaging Platform asks for your consent where required, such as in the EEA, UK and some US states.

Firebase

Scanora uses Firebase Crashlytics, Analytics, Performance Monitoring, Remote Config and Cloud Messaging to find crashes, measure speed, understand basic feature use, adjust app settings and deliver notifications.

Google Play Billing

Premium purchases are processed by Google Play. Google handles payment and billing. Scanora receives only the purchased product and its status so it can switch ads off and restore your purchase.

Android sharing & external apps

If you share, open, email, dial, text, save or otherwise send content to another app, Android transfers the selected content to that app. The receiving app's privacy policy then applies.

YOUR CHOICES

Control your data

Scanora gives you practical controls over locally stored content, app permissions and sharing.

Delete local history and files+

You can remove individual Library items inside Scanora. Clearing Scanora's app data or uninstalling the app can remove app-private history, settings and Scanora-managed local files. Files you deliberately exported to another device location may remain there until you delete them.

Manage permissions+

You can review or revoke Scanora permissions through Android Settings. Some features, such as camera scanning, may not work when the required permission is denied.

Sharing and exporting+

Sharing and exporting are user-initiated. Before sending sensitive QR content, Wi-Fi credentials, contact details, OCR text or documents to another app or person, review the destination and content.

Retention+

Local Scanora data remains on your device until you delete it, clear app data or uninstall Scanora, subject to Android storage behavior. Diagnostics, analytics and performance data are kept for a limited period under our Firebase settings and Google's policies. Third-party Google services retain any data they independently process according to their own policies and settings.

Ads and your privacy choices+

Where the law requires it, Google asks for your consent before Scanora requests ads. You can review or change that choice in Scanora under Settings, Ad privacy choices (shown when required). You can also reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalization in Android Settings and at adssettings.google.com. Without personalization you still see ads, but they are less relevant. Premium removes ads completely.

Premium, subscriptions and refunds+

Premium is optional. Purchases and subscriptions are managed in Google Play, where you can cancel a subscription, check renewal dates and request refunds under Google Play's policies. If you reinstall Scanora or change phone, use Restore purchases on the Premium screen with the same Google account. A cancelled subscription keeps ads off until the paid period ends. You can also watch a short rewarded ad for a temporary ad-free period without buying anything.

Notifications+

Notifications about updates, offers, new features and service status are optional. Deny the permission when Android asks, or turn notifications off later in Android Settings, Apps, Scanora, Notifications.

Your rights+

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK, Switzerland, Pakistan or US states such as California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict data, to object to certain processing and to withdraw consent. Scanora does not keep accounts, so data held by Google services is linked to an installation or device identifier. Contact us with your request and we will help where we can, and we may point you to Google for data that Google controls. Where required, we rely on your consent for personalized ads, and on our legitimate interest in keeping Scanora stable, fast and improving it for diagnostics and basic usage statistics.

International processing+

Google processes data from ads, Firebase and Google Play in many countries, which may differ from where you live. Google describes its safeguards in its privacy policy and Firebase privacy documentation.

Changes to this policy+

We update this page when Scanora's data practices change and show the date at the top. 29 Oct 2026: added Google AdMob ads and consent, Firebase (Crashlytics, Analytics, Performance Monitoring, Remote Config, Cloud Messaging), notifications and Google Play Billing purchases. 9 Oct 2026: first version (no ads or analytics).

SECURITY

Reasonable safeguards

Scanora uses Android platform security, scoped storage where applicable and app-private storage for managed data. No mobile app or storage system can guarantee absolute security, so protect your device and review sensitive content before sharing it.

CHILDREN

General audience app

Scanora is a general-purpose scanning utility and is not specifically directed to children. Scanora does not require account registration or intentionally request profile information from children. Ads are served by Google under its policies. If you believe a child has provided personal information through Scanora, contact us and we will help.
